A LITTLE SMOKE. A FEW MIRRORS.

A concept,
with chemistry.

This is an interactive design prototype by Andy J Myers. All matches and replies are invented. Postcode searches re-label an illustrative map; they do not look up real people or geographical positions.

No form data, pictures, messages or payments are submitted. Selected pictures and keepsakes exist only in page memory. The live service would require accounts, secure messaging, payment processing and operational support.

The idea: £25 once; pseudonymous adult profiles; 14-day messages; 100 keepsakes. Discretion is a goal, not a promise that nobody can identify you or copy an exchange.
