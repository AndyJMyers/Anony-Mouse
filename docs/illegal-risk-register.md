# Initial illegal-content risk register — 17 October 2026

Companion to the [age/access assessment](age-access-assessment.md). UK-linked adult introductions, non-explicit profiles/text/images, private accepted requests, district discovery, £10 manually purchased months. No real service/user evidence yet. These are conservative pre-launch design judgments, not an operator-signed completed compliance programme or measured residual risk.

Method: consider likely misuse and possible severity on this specific ungated/unmoderated planned design. L/M/H likelihood and impact yield an initial risk, with uncertainty favouring caution. User-generated media, private stranger contact, pseudonyms, sexual intentions, sparse districts and planned rapid national growth matter. No algorithmic engagement feed, streaming, patron payouts or group messaging are planned. The absence of those features reduces some pathways; it does not exclude them.

The June 2026 Ofcom risk guidance now lists **18** priority kinds, including cyberflashing; older project references to 17 should not be used as a current checklist. Grooming, CSAM files and CSAM links are considered separately within CSEA below. Include attempts, repeat behaviour, indirect links, vulnerable adults, children bypassing the gate and migration off service.

| Kind | Likelihood / impact → initial risk | Pathway and planned mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Terrorism | L / H → M | Recruitment/propaganda links in bios/DMs; report/escalation and removal. |
| CSEA: grooming | M / H → H | Minor enters or adult pretends to be a minor; validated gate, urgent investigation, NCA reporting. |
| CSEA: imagery | M / H → H | Prohibited attachment; restricted evidence access/preservation and statutory report. |
| CSEA: URLs | M / H → H | Links used to evade media limits; include links in report handling. |
| Hate | M / H → H | Profiles expose sexual orientation and other traits; report, block, investigate and restrict. |
| Harassment, stalking, threats, abuse | H / H → H | Repeated unwanted contact, district/photo identification; approximate location, accepted requests, blocks, anti-evasion. |
| Controlling/coercive behaviour | M / H → H | Manipulation or threats in private exchanges; prioritised reports, evidence and safety escalation. |
| Intimate image abuse | M / H → H | Images posted without depicted person's agreement, threats/deepfakes; removal and restricted review. |
| Extreme pornography | L / H → M | Shock uploads/links despite non-explicit rule; prompt removal and escalation. |
| Sexual exploitation of adults | M / H → H | Coerced introductions, third-party controlled accounts; investigate credible indicators and referrals. |
| Human trafficking | L / H → M | Coerced/proxy profiles and arranged meetings; same escalation, not a no-risk assumption. |
| Unlawful immigration | L / M → L | Private facilitation messages unrelated to purpose; prohibited illegal-use rule and reports. |
| Fraud/financial offences | H / H → H | Romance fraud, impersonation and payment requests; clear warnings, reports and repeat-abuse controls. |
| Proceeds of crime | L / H → M | Money-mule solicitation; no patron transfers/payouts and escalation. |
| Drugs/psychoactive substances | M / H → H | Solicitation or supply associated with meeting; illegal-use rule and rapid reports. |
| Weapons | L / H → M | Threats/sales links; report and immediate safety escalation where credible. |
| Encouraging/assisting suicide or serious self-harm | L / H → M | Coercion after rejection; urgent reporting/escalation. |
| Foreign interference | L / H → M | Targeted manipulation/blackmail, including office-holder audiences; confidential escalation and account restrictions. |
| Animal cruelty | L / H → M | Prohibited shock media; removal, restricted evidence and escalation. |
| Cyberflashing | H / H → H | Unsolicited sexual images after a hello is accepted; acceptance is not sexual/image consent. Ban explicit content, enable immediate report/block and sanctions. |
| Other illegal content | M / H → H | Impersonation, privacy offences, unlawful disclosure or emerging misuse; case-by-case legal assessment rather than asserting every offensive message is criminal. |

No residual-risk score is assigned before implementation evidence. Existing local report/block/expiry functionality does not deliver staffed incident handling, enforceable suspension/removal, lawful evidence preservation, appeals or production anti-abuse controls. Rapid removal alone does not satisfy prevention duties. Consider applicable code measures and document any alternative; small size is not an automatic exemption. A service with several medium/high risks may attract additional measures even without millions of users.

Next evidence to record: responsible operator; source-by-source review of Ofcom risk factors/register; specific current-code applicability and any alternative measures; independent review where appropriate; provider obligations; responder coverage/training; request-to-action and recurrence metrics; tested evidence deletion and NCA workflow. Record owner approval and review on actual implementation, new risk evidence or material changes before launch. No moderator tooling or deployment is authorised by preparing this register alone.
